# Changelog

## 0.2.0 | 2026-11-25

* A guided dashboard for importing a song, separating stems, and transcribing a selected track.
* MIDI reference parts can be added to an open session and applied to audio stems.
* English interface, light and dark themes, and volume controls.
* A moving score playhead and smooth auto-scroll during playback.
* Loop ranges, a beat-aligned count-in, and a metronome with independent volume and timing adjustment.
* Tablature rhythm marks below the strings and PDF export for the full score.
* Editable ghost notes, dead notes, slides into notes, and palm mute marks.

## 0.1.0 | 2026-11-25

* Local import of audio, MIDI, and saved sessions.
* Six-track separation with full-mix, solo, and custom backing playback.
* Transcription for pitched instruments, bass, and drums. Custom tuning, a rhythm grid, and manual note editing.
* Interactive tablature, piano and percussion notation, playback speed, and looping.
* Portable sessions with audio and playback settings. MIDI, MusicXML, text tablature, and WAV exports.
